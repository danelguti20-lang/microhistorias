Privacy Policy – Micro Historias Uploader

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the application Micro Historias Uploader (“the App”) handles information when it accesses the YouTube Data API.

1. Purpose and users of the App

The App is used exclusively by the owner of the YouTube channel Micro Historias to upload and schedule videos on their own channel through an automation built on Make. The App is not offered to the public and is not used by any other person.

2. Data collection

The App does not collect, store, or share any data from other users or from other YouTube channels. It only interacts with the channel owner’s own YouTube account in order to upload and schedule videos.

3. OAuth tokens

The OAuth tokens granted by the channel owner are stored only within Make, and are used solely to authorize video uploads to the owner’s channel. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

4. Data retention and deletion

The App does not retain any data of its own. Videos and their metadata (titles, descriptions, scheduling details) are stored only on YouTube.

The channel owner can remove the App’s access at any time by revoking its permissions at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and deleting the corresponding connection in Make.

Any data deletion request can be sent to danelguti20@gmail.com and will be handled within a maximum of 7 days.

5. YouTube API Services and Google Privacy Policy

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms).

Google Privacy Policy: for information on how Google collects and handles data, see the Google Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact: danelguti20@gmail.com
